Researched 2026-10-21 for the offer: "I help health systems turn messy data into reliable reporting on Microsoft Fabric: faster loads, fewer manual fixes, one governed platform."

**Labels**
- **Verified**: primary source (the organisation that ran the survey, or the named person quoted in an interview its publisher ran).
- **Reported**: secondary coverage, a vendor or consultancy summary, or a search-engine excerpt of a page I could not open.
- **Inference**: my reasoning from the evidence. It is not a finding.

**Limits of this research (read first)**
- Reddit (r/healthIT, r/PowerBI, r/MicrosoftFabric, r/BusinessIntelligence) is blocked to the research tools ("domains are not accessible to our user agent"). As a result, **no Reddit quotes are included**. Practitioner language below comes from named executives, KLAS, HFMA, and the Microsoft Fabric Community forum.

### Buying-committee map (Inference)
Built from the rows above. Not a sourced finding.
- **Signs for a platform decision:** CIO, CDAO or Chief Digital Officer. CFO involvement is likely when the case rests on cost or finance reporting.
- **Signs or recommends a services engagement for a solo consultant:** VP or Director of Enterprise Analytics / BI.
- **Champion:** Director or manager of data engineering or BI. This is the person who owns refresh failures and the report backlog.
- **Does the work:** Clarity/Caboodle report writers, BI developers, data engineers.
- **Blocks or vetoes:** CISO / privacy (PHI), Epic analytics (Cogito) team, enterprise architecture.

## 3. PAINS: their own words (hook material)

Exact quotes only, each with its source. Speakers are labelled by type.

Source: [Chartis](https://www.chartis.com/insights/last-mile-data-analytics-how-achieve-roi-healthcare), 2025-01-03.

## 4. HOW THEY BUY

### Why they hire services firms
- **Verified.** KLAS *Data & Analytics Services 2025* (17 purchase decisions, Aug 2023 – Jun 2025) says health systems hire firms to "help design and develop data and analytics strategies" and "provide short-term expertise and project assistance." Health systems are prioritising these services to organise clinical and operational data and to get ready for AI. Selection factors sit behind the KLAS login. Source: [KLAS](https://klasresearch.com/report/data-and-analytics-services-2025-a-decision-insights-market-analysis/3886), 2025-08-29.

### What they reward in vendors
- **Reported (KLAS via HIT Consultant, 2025-05-02).** Customers reward vendors that "act as proactive partners." Example: a "responsive partner that understands their goals and guides them proactively." They penalise staff turnover, lack of expertise, lack of operational context, unclear roadmaps, and storage costs.

### Budget and ROI
- **Reported (Bain/KLAS via Healthcare Dive, 2026-09-24).** Organisations with formal ROI thresholds expect about 3–4× return. "ROI will become increasingly important to sustain investment."
- **Reported (Becker's).** AI and tech spend is increasingly co-owned across finance, operations and clinical leaders.

### Buying triggers (evidence found)
1. **Epic's analytics move to Cogito Cloud on Azure / Microsoft Fabric.**
   - Reported (RSM, 2026-04-21): Epic plans to "release all new data models exclusively through the Cogito Cloud." RSM calls this "a significant structural shift, rather than a feature update."
   - Reported (Hakkoda, undated): at UGM 2023 Epic said it would rebuild Cogito on an Azure-native lakehouse. Hakkoda flags reliance on Fabric as a risk. Source: [Hakkoda](https://hakkoda.io/resources/moving-to-azure/).
   - Reported (EHC Consulting, undated): Cogito Cloud, formerly Nebula, is built on Azure and leverages Fabric. Source: [EHC Consulting](https://ehc.consulting/epic-on-azure-strategy-deployment-models-and-what-you-need-to-know/).
   - I did **not** find Epic's own primary announcement.
2. **Epic-on-Azure hosting migrations.**
   - Reported (Microsoft customer story, vendor): Franciscan Health reports $45M saved over five years. Source: [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/25710-franciscan-health-azure-virtual-machines).
   - Reported (Microsoft blog, vendor, 2026-03-19): an unnamed organisation saved "$4 million on analytics alone by replacing legacy data analytics tools with Microsoft Fabric." Source: [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/microsoft-in-business/business-transformation/2026/03/19/delivering-more-cost-efficient-healthcare-with-epic-on-azure/).
3. **M&A and consolidation.**
   - Verified: Kaufman Hall counted 46 announced hospital/health-system deals in 2025, with $18.5B transacted revenue (the lowest on record) and a record 43.5% involving a financially distressed party. Q4 2025 had 17 deals, including 4 mega-mergers. Source: [Kaufman Hall](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/hospital-and-health-system-ma-activity-ends-2025-momentum), 2026-01-15.
   - Reported: 22 deals in Q1 2026 (the most since early 2020) and 18 in Q2 2026. Source: [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/kaufman-hall-reports-hospital-manda-decline-in-2025-amid-financial-stress-and-policy-uncertainty-2026-01-21) and [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/were-witnessing-proactive-positioning-over-reactive-consolidation-50-things-to-know-about-hospital-ma-in-2026/), via search excerpt.
   - Inference: every merger creates two of everything (EHR instances, warehouses, dashboards) and a reporting reconciliation problem.
4. **Legacy consolidation, archiving and EHR migration.** 71% put legacy consolidation and archiving in their top-3 data-management priorities, and 57% plan higher spend on EHR migrations. Reported (Harmony/CHIME, small n).
5. **AI initiative and AI governance.** AI is the top tech priority for 57% (Reported). Leaders recommend "investing in data infrastructure and readiness" to scale (Chartis, Verified).
6. **New CDAO hire, failed audit, or Synapse/legacy SQL retirement.** **No evidence found** in this research. No Synapse retirement claim was verified, so don't use one in content without a primary Microsoft source.

### Objections (evidence found)
- **"We're an Epic shop."** Over half of providers would prefer an EHR-native clinical tool with basic or partial functionality over a third-party solution. Reported (Bain/KLAS via Healthcare Dive, 2026-09-24).
- **Security and PHI.** Cybersecurity and compliance is the #1 data-management priority (86% top-3, Reported) and leads CIO initiative rankings (CereCore, Reported).
- **Cost and forecastability.** Consumption-based pricing makes TCO harder to forecast (Reid Health CIO, Reported). Microsoft's move to consumption pricing makes forecasting harder (Hakkoda, vendor, Reported). Storage-cost frustration with an analytics vendor (KLAS, Reported).
- **Microsoft lock-in.** Hakkoda flags "reliance on Microsoft Fabric" as a key risk of Epic's Cogito move (Reported, vendor).
- **Staff skills.** KLAS cites "internal staffing limits" and Epic training as barriers (Reported). Scottsdale: insufficient in-house expertise 14% (Verified).
- **Accountability.** CIOs will outsource "capacity and commodity work" but not "strategy, architecture and accountability" (Reported).
- Inference: position as capacity and expertise under their architecture, not as a replacement for their judgement.

### Proof they want
- KLAS-style peer validation. KLAS scores and Best in KLAS are widely cited, for example the [2025 Best in KLAS Data & Analytics Platforms](https://klasresearch.com/best-in-klas-ranking/data-and-analytics-platforms/2025/97) and Power BI as Best in KLAS for Data Visualization & Reporting. Reported via search excerpt.
- **Proofs of concept.** RSM recommends validating value "through Microsoft-funded proofs of concept before committing to broader programs" (Reported, consultancy, 2026-04-21).
- **ROI against a stated threshold** of 3–4× (Reported, Bain/KLAS).
- **Peer-shared lessons in vendor-free settings.** HDAA was founded in 2001 as a place to share analytics lessons "in a vendor-free environment" (Reported).
- Inference: a named peer health system or a before/after metric (refresh time, request backlog, hours of manual fixes) will carry more weight than a feature list.

  - 79% are more likely to champion proposals from vendors who consistently publish high-quality thought leadership.
  - Sources: [Edelman](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/business-marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report), [DemandGen Report](https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/marketers-need-to-gear-content-to-hidden-b2b-buyers-edelman-linkedin/49901/), [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/edelman-linkedin-thought-leadership-report/). These are search excerpts, and the 95% figure is reported inconsistently, so don't use it.
- Inference: named health-system leaders are visible on Becker's lists and podcasts and in CHIME programming, which suggests a public, professional-profile culture. That is not proof of LinkedIn engagement.

### What makes vendors and consultants sound untrustworthy (evidence)
| Signal | Label / source |
|---|---|
| Lack of expertise and **staff turnover** | Reported (KLAS via HIT Consultant, 2025-05-02) |
