What changes in the strategy

The original calendar was demand-informed, not proven to have an unmet content market. This audit adds supply evidence. It does not establish ranking potential, search volume, audience willingness to buy, or completeness of all answers online.

  1. Generic tutorials face substantial overlap. Capacity diagnosis, tool selection, watermark loading, security setup, data quality, planning and agent-cost monitoring all have existing answers. Some also have code, notebooks and real examples.
  2. Operational proof is a contribution to test. A narrow scenario with seed data, expected outputs, a failure injection and an observed recovery can give readers more usable evidence. This is an editorial hypothesis, not a claim that all competitors lack these assets.
  3. Industry gaps are less certain. Healthcare and education offer useful context, but low-quality or generic search matches are not evidence of unmet demand. Preserve the original small sample sizes; validate the exact workflow with practitioners before expanding.
  4. Freshness can invalidate the premise. Shortcut lifecycle support, deployment plans and SQL compatibility have current documentation. Publish dated regression results instead of repeating old unsupported-feature claims.

Supply side

Reading depth

Boundaries

Search discovery is labeled provisional. The audit is English-language and search-led, without paid courses, private communities, complete channel catalogs or full comment histories. A missed result cannot prove absence. Search positions are not tracked rankings, and keyword candidates have no measured volume or difficulty.

Coverage tests: Does the source answer the same task and constraint? Does it demonstrate a result? Does it include recovery, boundaries and usable assets? Is its platform version still applicable? Is the proposed contribution more than a new title or industry label?

Priority method: qualitative fixed-cohort review. Pilot = selected task with demand linkage and a concrete proof artifact; Next = a useful differentiated extension; Validate = exact audience/workflow or coverage remains uncertain; Lower = consolidate or defer generic overlap; Watch = release-dependent. No fabricated numerical opportunity score or demand-weighted coverage percentage.
