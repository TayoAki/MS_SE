# Story bank — the real moments authority videos are built from

Authority videos ("how I did it") only work with YOUR stories: a real problem, what you did, what changed. Claude never
invents a story, a client, a number or a result. This file grows the easy way: Claude interviews you once (5 stories at
onboarding), and after any session where you mention something that happened, it asks "add this to the story bank?".

## Rules for health-system stories
- **No identifiers.** No client name, hospital name, city, patient detail, or anything that lets a viewer work out who it
  was — unless the client approved it in writing (note where the approval lives).
- **Real numbers only, with where they came from** ("refresh went from ~4 h to ~20 min, measured in the refresh history").
  If you don't remember the number, the story says "much faster", not a made-up figure.
- **Your part, honestly.** What you did, what the team did, what didn't work.

## Interview questions (Claude asks these, a few at a time)
1. The worst reporting fire you've walked into — what was broken, who was hurting, what did you do first?
2. A time two reports showed different numbers for the same thing — how did you find out which was right?
3. A refresh or load that kept failing — what was the real cause?
4. A Fabric (or Power BI) cost or capacity surprise you've seen — what caused it, what fixed it?
5. A security or access question that scared a team — "who can see this?" — and how you answered it.
6. A manual process (Excel patch, copy-paste, weekend fix) you removed — how many hours did it take before?
7. A migration (SSRS, Synapse, on-prem SQL, Excel) — what surprised you?
8. Something you believed about Fabric that turned out to be wrong.
9. Why you do this work — the moment you decided health data was your niche.
10. A mistake you made and what you do differently now.

## Stories
Template — copy for each new story:

```
### <short title>                                   pillar: P1–P5 · added: <date>
- Situation: <who (anonymised), what was broken, why it mattered>
- What I did: <the steps, in plain words>
- Result: <what changed — real numbers with their source, or plain words if no number>
- The lesson / mic drop: <one line>
- Can show on screen: <synthetic rebuild, diagram, a screen recording with demo data — never the client's data>
- Approval: <none needed (anonymised) | written approval: where>
- Used in: <script folders>
```
