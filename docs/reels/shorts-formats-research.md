# Short-form formats study: data, BI and tech Shorts (pulled 2026-10-22)

Method: `.claude/skills/niche-scripts/scripts/shorts.py`. Each channel's /shorts tab was listed (up to 120 latest). The ratio (×) is a Short's views divided by the median views of that channel's listed Shorts. Frames came from i.ytimg.com: the first frame, and YouTube's own frames at about 25, 50 and 75 % of the video. Formats were tagged by looking at those frames (F1–F6 are defined in `content-machine/formats.md`). Limits: YouTube only; no lengths and no transcripts, because video pages are blocked from the cloud container; views as of the pull date.
