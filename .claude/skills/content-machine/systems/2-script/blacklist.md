# Starter blacklist — words and patterns that make a script sound written by AI

Use it with the user's own list (`~/content-machine/voice/blacklist.md`, which wins). Replace with the plain version or cut.

## Words
| Never | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "game-changer", "revolutionary", "next level", "insane" (as filler) | say what it actually does |
| "unlock", "unleash", "supercharge", "elevate", "empower" | "lets you", "helps you", the concrete result |
| "delve", "dive into", "navigate the landscape", "in today's fast-paced world" | cut it |
| "seamless(ly)", "robust", "cutting-edge", "leverage" | plain words: "easy", "solid", "new", "use" |
| "quietly launched / quietly killed" | "just launched", "just removed" |
| "under the hood" | "how it works" (or drop it) |
| jargon your viewer wouldn't say ("endpoint", "deploy", "agentic", "quota") | the everyday word ("link", "put online", "AI that does tasks", "limit") |

## Patterns
- **"Here's the thing / here's the catch / here's what nobody tells you"** as a crutch → just say the thing.
- **"It's not just X, it's Y"** symmetry, rule-of-three adjectives, rhetorical questions answered right away.
- **Aspirational fluff:** "before you even open your laptop", "while you sip your coffee", "for the price of a coffee"
  → say the real time or the real price.
- **An unexplained spec in the hook** ("a 27-billion-parameter model") → lead with what it does for them; the spec later.
- **Hedging stacks** ("might potentially help you possibly") → one plain claim with its source.
- **Over-polished endings** ("the future is here", "the possibilities are endless") → end on the call to action.
