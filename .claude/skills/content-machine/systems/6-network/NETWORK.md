# System 6 — comments, DMs and signals (the active half of LinkedIn)

Videos are passive networking: people find you. This is the active half: you show up where your buyers already are. It is
done by the user, by hand, about 15 minutes a day. Claude prepares everything and sends nothing.

## Hard rules
- **No automation, ever.** LinkedIn's User Agreement bans bots and automation for comments, messages, connection requests
  and likes, and bans scraping tools; accounts get restricted for it. No engagement pods, no auto-DM tools, no scraping.
  Claude never logs into LinkedIn.
- **No pitch in a first comment.** The comment's job is to be useful and memorable; the headline under your name does the
  selling.
- Health data rules apply to comments too: no client names, no patient details, no compliance promises.

## The 15-minute routine
1. **Your own posts first (5 min).** Reply to every real comment, in your words. Each keyword comment → send the freebie
   by DM (Claude drafts the message: their first name, the freebie, one question about their situation). Log real
   questions in `~/content-machine/audience-questions.md`.
2. **Comment on 3–5 posts from the target list (8 min).** The user pastes a post (link + text). Claude drafts 2 options:
   address them by first name · add one specific thing (a test you ran, a number from the guide with its source, a
   counter-example) · end with an open question. 2–5 sentences, no links, no "great post". Early comments on a fresh post
   are seen most.
3. **One connection request (2 min)** — only after you have exchanged comments, with a note that refers to that
   exchange. Claude drafts it.

## The target list — `~/content-machine/network.md`
Three tiers, kept in a table (name · role · org · why · link · last touch):
- **Buyers and champions** at target health systems (titles from `icp.md`). Find them through the people page of the
  health system's LinkedIn company page, filtered by keyword (e.g. "analytics", "data", "Power BI"). Read their activity
  before commenting; if they only comment on others, answer one of their comments.
- **Voices your buyers follow:** Fabric and healthcare-data creators, Microsoft folks, MVPs (seeded from the research).
- **Communities:** the Health & Life Sciences Fabric User Group on the Fabric Community site (488 members in the Oct 2026
  research snapshot), r/MicrosoftFabric, the local Power BI / Fabric user group.

## Signals — `~/content-machine/signals.md`
A signal is a public sign a health system is about to need you: a job posting that mentions Microsoft Fabric, OneLake or
Direct Lake; a new CDAO / VP Analytics hire; an EHR migration or merger; a cloud or Synapse-retirement announcement; a
conference talk about their data platform. When the user asks "find signals", web-search for these, log each with its
link and date, and suggest one helpful, non-salesy touch (a comment on their post, a relevant video, a congratulations
with one useful thought). A signal is a reason to be helpful, never a reason to send a pitch.
