## Niche rule — no patient data on screen, ever
Health data is PHI. Every screen recording, screenshot or table on screen uses demo or synthetic data. If a frame shows
anything that could be a real patient name, MRN, date of birth or note, stop and ask — blur is not good enough, re-record.
